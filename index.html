Good to Know

04 / 05
What's the dress code?
Garden party / cocktail attire. Elegant but relaxed — you'll be outdoors for part of the night, so dress comfortably. Heels will meet grass. No black tie, no flip flops.
Indoors or outdoors?
Ceremony and cocktail hour are in the gardens. Dinner and dancing move inside. September in Mill Valley is usually perfect — warm, mild, a little breezy.
Can I bring a plus-one?
Plus-ones are noted on your invitation. If you're unsure, just reach out and we'll sort it out.
Are kids welcome?
We adore your children — and we're keeping this one adults-only so everyone can fully relax and enjoy the night. We hope you understand!
Is there parking?
Yes, parking is available on-site. We encourage carpooling and rideshares — the venue is just a short trip from downtown Mill Valley and Sausalito.
Dietary restrictions?
We'll have options for all dietary needs. Just note any restrictions in your RSVP and we'll make sure you're taken care of.
